Your Wila email signature

Three steps. It takes about a minute.

1Check your details

2Copy it

3Paste it into your mail app

Mail on an iPhone or iPad
  1. Open Settings, then Apps, then Mail, then Signature. On iOS 17 or earlier it is Settings, then Mail.
  2. If you have more than one email account, tap Per Account and find your heywila.com account.
  3. Delete what is in the box. Tap and hold in the empty box, then tap Paste.
  4. If the colors or sizes look wrong, shake your phone and tap Undo when it offers “Undo Change Attributes”.
  5. Send yourself a test email.
Gmail in a web browser
  1. In Gmail, click the gear at the top right, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll down to Signature and click Create new. Name it Wila.
  3. Click in the box on the right and paste.
  4. Under Signature defaults, choose Wila for new emails and for replies and forwards.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

The Gmail phone app uses this signature too, unless you have set a separate mobile signature in the app.

Mail on a Mac
  1. In Mail, open Mail, then Settings, then Signatures.
  2. Select your heywila.com account on the left and click +. Name it Wila.
  3. Untick Always match my default message font.
  4. Click in the box on the right, select everything in it, and paste.
  5. Next to Choose Signature, pick Wila. Close the window.

To change your name or title later, come back to this page, copy again, and paste over the old one. Editing the signature inside your mail app can break its layout.
